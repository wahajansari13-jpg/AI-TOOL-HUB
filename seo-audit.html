What Does an SEO Site Audit Check?

A complete SEO audit examines multiple layers of your website:

  • On-Page SEO – Title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure (H1–H3), keyword usage and content depth.
  • Technical SEO – HTTPS, crawlability signals, canonical tags, robots directives and indexability.
  • Mobile Usability – Viewport meta tag, responsive design signals and mobile-friendly structure.
  • Performance Signals – Page weight indicators and basic speed-related factors.
  • Content Quality – Thin content detection, missing headings and image alt attributes.
  • Security & Trust – SSL/HTTPS presence and basic security headers when detectable.

Results are grouped into Errors (critical), Warnings (important) and Passed checks so you know exactly what to fix first.

Why Regular SEO Audits Matter in 2026

Search engines and AI systems continuously update how they evaluate websites. Issues that were minor last year can become ranking barriers today. Regular audits help you:

  • Catch broken technical foundations early
  • Keep titles and meta descriptions optimized for click-through rate
  • Ensure mobile users have a good experience
  • Maintain content quality and avoid thin pages
  • Stay ready for AI search and generative engine optimization

Common SEO Issues This Tool Detects

  • Missing or poorly written title tags
  • Missing meta descriptions
  • No H1 or multiple H1 tags
  • Missing viewport meta (mobile problems)
  • HTTP instead of HTTPS
  • Images without alt text
  • Very thin content
  • Missing canonical signals

How to Improve Your SEO Health Score

After running the audit, focus on these high-impact actions:

  1. Fix all critical Errors first (HTTPS, missing titles, major crawl blocks).
  2. Write unique, compelling title tags (50–60 characters) and meta descriptions (120–160 characters).
  3. Ensure every important page has one clear H1 and logical heading hierarchy.
  4. Add descriptive alt text to all meaningful images.
  5. Make the entire site mobile-responsive and fast.
  6. Expand thin pages with original, useful content.
  7. Re-run the audit after changes to track improvement.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is an SEO site audit?

An SEO site audit is a systematic check of your website for technical, on-page and content issues that can prevent search engines from ranking your pages well.

3. How is the SEO Health Score calculated?

The score is based on the number and severity of passed checks versus errors and warnings across on-page, technical, mobile and content factors.

4. Can I audit any website?

Yes. Enter any publicly accessible URL. Some advanced signals may be limited by browser CORS restrictions, but core on-page checks still work.

Run a full audit after major site changes and at least once a month to catch new issues early.

No. A high health score means your technical and on-page foundation is solid. Rankings also depend on content quality, backlinks, competition and search intent match.

Always fix Errors first, then Warnings. The Priority Fixes section in the report shows the recommended order.

10. Is my data stored?
