How to convert marla to square feet

Marla and kanal are traditional land units used in Pakistan and parts of India. This converter changes them into square feet, square yards, square meters and acres, so you can compare plot sizes in the unit you need.

Marla to sq ft

Multiply marla by 272.25. Example: 5 marla × 272.25 = 1,361.25 sq ft.

Kanal to marla

Multiply kanal by 20. Example: 2 kanal × 20 = 40 marla.

Kanal to sq ft

Multiply kanal by 5,445. Example: 1 kanal = 5,445 sq ft.

Sq ft to marla

Divide square feet by 272.25. Example: 2,722.5 ÷ 272.25 = 10 marla.

Which marla size should I use?

The standard marla is 272.25 sq ft (30.25 sq yards). Some private housing societies sell plots using a 225 sq ft marla. Check your plot file or society rules, then pick the matching size in the “Marla size” menu.
