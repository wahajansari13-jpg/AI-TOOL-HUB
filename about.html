About Browser Tools Hub

Browser Tools Hub is a free collection of simple, fast, and private micro-tools that run entirely in your browser.

We built this site because most online tools force you to create an account, upload your files to their servers, or show aggressive ads. We believe useful tools should be instant, private, and free.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality browser-based utilities that respect your privacy. Every tool processes data locally on your device — nothing is uploaded to our servers.

What Makes Us Different

  • 100% client-side processing (your data never leaves your device)
  • No account or signup required
  • No tracking of personal files
  • Clean, fast, and mobile-friendly interface
  • Completely free to use
