About Browser Tools Hub

A small collection of free tools for everyday tasks like converting documents, compressing images and formatting JSON, built to work without an account.

Why this site exists

Simple jobs such as turning a Word file into a PDF or shrinking a photo often send you to sites that ask you to sign up, upload your file to a server, or pay after the first use. Browser Tools Hub was started to offer these everyday tools in one place, with no signup and, wherever possible, with the work done inside your own browser.

The site is new and still growing. We add and improve tools based on what people actually need, so feedback is welcome.

Who runs it

Browser Tools Hub is an independent project. You can reach the person behind it at support@browsertools.site or through the Contact page.

How your data is handled

Most tools, including the Document Converter, Image Compressor, PDF Page Remover, Password Generator and JSON Formatter, run in your browser, so the files and text you use are not uploaded to our servers.

Two tools are different by nature. The Email Generator uses a third-party temporary mail service to receive messages, and the Speed Test connects to external test servers. Anything sent to a temporary inbox can be read by anyone who knows the address, so do not use it for important accounts. Read the full Privacy Policy for details on cookies and third-party services.

Our tools

  • Document Converter – Word, Excel and text files to PDF
  • Image Compressor – reduce JPG, PNG and WebP file size
  • PDF Page Remover – delete pages from a PDF with previews
  • QR Code Generator – QR codes for links, WiFi and contacts
  • JSON Formatter – format, validate and minify JSON
  • Password Generator – random passwords using the browser's crypto API
  • Case Converter – UPPERCASE, Title Case, camelCase and slugs
  • Word Counter – words, characters and reading time
  • Lorem Ipsum Generator – placeholder text for layouts
  • Text Summarizer – extractive summary of long text
  • SEO Site Audit – basic on-page and technical SEO checks
  • AdSense Checker – a checklist-style readiness review
  • Speed Test – download, upload and ping
  • Email Generator – temporary inbox for verification emails

The Text Summarizer is an algorithm that picks the most important sentences from your text. It is not a generative AI model.

Quality and corrections

We try to describe each tool honestly, including its limits. Browser-based conversion can differ from desktop software, for example complex Word layouts may not convert perfectly. If you find a bug, a wrong explanation or a broken link, please tell us and we will fix it.
