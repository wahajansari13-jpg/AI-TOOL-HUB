How Extractive & Abstractive Summarization Works

Modern text reduction technology relies on two main approaches:

  • Extractive Summarization: Evaluates sentence weights, keyword densities, and positional hierarchy to extract the most significant sentences directly from the document.
  • Abstractive Summarization: Synthesizes core concepts and rephrases them into fresh, human-readable prose.

This tool uses the extractive method. It does not use generative AI, so it never invents new sentences. It scores each sentence by how often its important words appear in your text and by its position, then returns the top-ranked sentences in their original order.
