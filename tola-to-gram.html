How to convert tola to grams

Tola is a traditional weight unit used in Pakistan, India and the Gulf to buy and sell gold and silver. Shops quote prices per tola, while international rates use grams or troy ounces. This converter changes between them instantly.

Tola to grams

Multiply tola by 11.664. Example: 5 tola × 11.664 = 58.32 g.

Grams to tola

Divide grams by 11.664. Example: 100 g ÷ 11.664 = 8.573 tola.

Tola to masha

Multiply tola by 12. Example: 2 tola = 24 masha.

Masha to grams

Multiply masha by 0.972. Example: 6 masha × 0.972 = 5.832 g.

Weight formulas

  • 1 tola = 11.664 g = 12 masha = 96 ratti
  • 1 masha = 0.972 g = 8 ratti
  • 1 ratti = 0.1215 g
  • 1 troy oz = 31.1035 g = 2.667 tola
  • 1 kg = 85.73 tola

Gold is also described by purity (karat). This tool converts weight only, so check the karat and the current market rate before you buy or sell.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams are in 1 tola?

How many masha are in 1 tola?

How many grams are in 10 tola?

How many tola are in 1 troy ounce?

1 troy ounce (31.1035 g) equals about 2.667 tola.
