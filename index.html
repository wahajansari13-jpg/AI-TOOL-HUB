How to Use Tools - Frequently Asked Questions

    Aap drag-and-drop feature ka use kar sakte hain ya fir file selection button par click kar sakte hain.

    Apne browser cache ko clear karein ya page ko refresh karke dobara koshish karein.

  7. How to clear cookies and ad preferences?
    Aap apne browser ki settings mein jaakar cookies ko easily delete kar sakte hain.
  8. How to contact support if assistance is needed?
    Aap humare contact page par jakar apni query bhej sakte hain.
  9. How to check for new tool updates and features?
    Humare homepage par regular visit karein taaki naye updates mil sakein.
