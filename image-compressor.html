Best Practices for Image Compression

For Websites

Use 70-80% quality and max width of 1920px. This keeps images sharp while loading fast.

For Social Media

Instagram and Facebook compress images anyway. Use 75-85% quality for best results.

For WhatsApp / Messaging

Lower quality (60-70%) is usually fine since apps re-compress images.

For Printing

Keep quality at 90%+ and avoid resizing. High resolution is more important.
