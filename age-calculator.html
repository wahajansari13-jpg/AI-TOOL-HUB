How to calculate age from date of birth

Your exact age is the time between your date of birth and a chosen date. This calculator uses the real calendar, so leap years and different month lengths are handled for you. By default it uses today’s date, and you can change it to any other date.

Age in years, months and days

Subtract the birth year, month and day from the chosen date. If the days are negative, borrow the days of the previous month. If the months are negative, borrow one year.

Total days lived

The number of days between the two dates. Example: someone born on 1 January 2000 is 366 days old on 1 January 2001 because 2000 was a leap year.

Next birthday

The calculator finds your next birthday date, counts the days left and shows the weekday it falls on.

Where is an age calculator useful?

  • School and college admission forms
  • Job applications with age limits
  • Passport, visa and CNIC form checks
  • Insurance and pension planning
  • Finding a child’s age in months

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my age from my date of birth?

Enter your date of birth and the calculator subtracts it from today’s date, giving your age in years, months and days.

How many days old am I?

The calculator shows your total age in days, weeks, months and hours along with your age in years.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the “Age at” date to any day. This is useful for school admission, job forms and legal age checks.

How is the exact age in months and days counted?

Complete years are counted first, then complete months, and the remaining days. Month lengths are taken from the calendar.

How do I find out when my next birthday is?

The result shows the number of days left until your next birthday and which day of the week it falls on.
