Free Google AdSense Approval Checker – Is Your Site Ready?

Getting approved for Google AdSense can be frustrating when you keep getting rejected without clear reasons. This free AdSense approval checker helps you evaluate your website against the most common requirements before you submit an application.

Enter your domain, run the analysis, and receive a clear eligibility score along with specific recommendations. The tool looks at technical setup, required legal pages, content signals, mobile readiness and security basics that Google reviewers care about.

What This AdSense Checker Analyzes

  • HTTPS / SSL certificate presence
  • Required pages (Privacy Policy, About, Contact, Terms)
  • Mobile-friendly viewport and responsive design signals
  • Basic SEO elements (title, meta description, headings)
  • Domain structure and accessibility
  • Content and navigation indicators

How to Improve Your AdSense Approval Chance

  1. Publish original, useful content (aim for 15–25 quality pages).
  2. Create clear Privacy Policy, About Us and Contact pages.
  3. Make sure the site is fully mobile responsive and fast.
  4. Use HTTPS everywhere.
  5. Avoid thin, duplicate or policy-violating content.
  6. Submit your site to Google Search Console and fix indexing issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a high score guarantee AdSense approval?

No. This tool evaluates common technical and structural requirements. Final approval is decided by Google and also depends on content quality, originality and policy compliance that only human reviewers can fully judge.

Is this tool free?

Yes. Completely free with no signup or limits.

Can I check any website?

Yes. Enter any public website URL. Results are generated based on publicly available signals and common AdSense requirements.

How often should I re-check?

Re-run the checker after making major changes (new pages, policy updates, redesign) before submitting or re-applying to AdSense.
