How to convert feet to inches, cm and meters

A length converter changes a measurement from one unit to another. This tool covers the units people search for most: feet, inches, centimeters, meters, millimeters, kilometers, yards, miles and nautical miles. Every result uses exact international definitions, so the numbers match what you get from a calculator or a tape measure.

Feet to inches

Multiply feet by 12. Example: 5.5 ft × 12 = 66 in.

Feet to cm

Multiply feet by 30.48. Example: 6 ft × 30.48 = 182.88 cm.

Feet to meters

Multiply feet by 0.3048. Example: 10 ft × 0.3048 = 3.048 m.

Inches to cm

Multiply inches by 2.54. Example: 10 in × 2.54 = 25.4 cm.

CM to feet and inches

Divide cm by 30.48 to get feet. Keep the whole number as feet, then multiply the decimal part by 12 to get inches. Example: 170 cm ÷ 30.48 = 5.577 ft, which is 5 ft 6.9 in.

Meters to feet

Multiply meters by 3.28084. Example: 2 m × 3.28084 = 6.562 ft.

Common length conversion formulas

  • 1 ft = 12 in = 30.48 cm = 0.3048 m
  • 1 in = 2.54 cm = 25.4 mm
  • 1 m = 100 cm = 3.28084 ft = 39.3701 in
  • 1 yd = 3 ft = 0.9144 m
  • 1 mi = 5,280 ft = 1.609344 km
