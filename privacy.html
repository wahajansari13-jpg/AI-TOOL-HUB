Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Executive Summary: Our Zero-Server Upload Guarantee

At Browser Tools Hub (accessible from browsertools.site), user privacy is our absolute priority. Unlike conventional web utility platforms that upload your files to external remote cloud servers, our tools operate under a 100% Client-Side Architecture. All file conversions, text formatting, image compressions, and encryption calculations take place directly in your web browser memory. Your sensitive data never leaves your physical device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because our web utilities run locally on your device, we do not collect, intercept, inspect, or store any of the following user assets:

  • User Documents & Files: Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PDF documents, or plain text uploaded to our Document Converter or PDF Page Remover are processed strictly in RAM.
  • Media Files: JPG, PNG, or WebP images compressed using our Image Compressor are never uploaded to remote servers.
  • Sensitive Strings & Inputs: Code payloads formatted in the JSON Formatter or generated keys in the Password Generator remain inside your browser instance.

2. Passive Information Collection & Web Analytics

To maintain operational stability, analyze site speed performance, and optimize user experience, we may collect non-personally identifiable technical information through standard web analytics integrations (such as Google Analytics).

This automated technical log data includes:

  • Anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and operating system environment
  • Referring site URLs and page dwell durations
  • Device viewport resolution and language preferences

This data is aggregated statistically and cannot be linked back to individual identity profiles.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies Policy

Browser Tools Hub may display third-party advertisements provided by networks such as Google AdSense to fund platform hosting costs.

Ad networks may use web cookies, web beacons, or tracking technologies to serve relevant ads based on prior website visits. Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or by adjusting your web browser cookie preferences.

4. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Compliance Rights

We respect international data privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

The Right to Access & Erasure: Because we do not harvest or store personal identity databases or user file payloads, we do not hold personal profile records to disclose or delete.

Do Not Sell My Personal Information: Browser Tools Hub does not sell, rent, or trade user data or usage records to third-party data brokers.

5. External Web Links

Our website may contain hyperlinked references to external sites. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party domains. We advise users to review the privacy documentation of any external platform they visit.

6. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page alongside a revised "Last Updated" timestamp. Continual use of our services after policy updates indicates acceptance of revised terms.
