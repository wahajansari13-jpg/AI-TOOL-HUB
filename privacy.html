Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Executive Summary: Our Zero-Server Upload Guarantee

At Browser Tools Hub (accessible from browsertools.site), user privacy is our absolute priority. Unlike conventional web utility platforms that upload your files to external remote cloud servers, many file and text utilities process the content you select locally in your browser. This does not apply to every feature: the temporary email tool connects to Guerrilla Mail, and some pages load scripts or libraries from third-party content delivery networks. See the third-party services section below.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

For tools that process files locally, the selected file content is handled in your browser rather than uploaded to a Browser Tools file-processing server. This statement does not cover third-party services used by specific features:

  • User Documents & Files: Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PDF documents, or plain text uploaded to our Document Converter or PDF Page Remover are processed strictly in RAM.
  • Media Files: JPG, PNG, or WebP images compressed using our Image Compressor are processed locally by the image-compression feature; the page may load its software dependencies from third-party CDNs.
  • Sensitive Strings & Inputs: Code payloads formatted in the JSON Formatter or generated keys in the Password Generator are processed by the relevant browser-based tool. Avoid entering secrets into any website unless you have reviewed its code and providers.

2. Passive Information Collection & Web Analytics

Our hosting provider may process standard server logs (such as request time, IP address, browser information and requested URL) for security, diagnostics and service delivery. This project archive does not show an active Google Analytics tracking tag. If analytics are added later, this policy should be updated to identify the provider and controls before deployment.

This automated technical log data includes:

  • Anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and operating system environment
  • Referring site URLs and page dwell durations
  • Device viewport resolution and language preferences

Server-log retention and access are governed by the hosting provider. We do not make a blanket claim that technical log data can never identify a visitor.

Third-Party Services and Temporary Email

The Temporary Email Generator connects to the Guerrilla Mail API at api.guerrillamail.com. When you create an address or check the inbox, request data is sent to that provider and incoming messages are handled under its own service rules. Temporary inboxes are not private or suitable for confidential information, banking, password recovery or important accounts. Review the provider's privacy terms before using this feature.

Some tools load third-party JavaScript libraries or styling resources from content delivery networks. Those providers may receive standard connection information when your browser requests their files. File processing may still occur locally after the library has loaded.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies Policy

Browser Tools Hub may display third-party advertisements provided by networks such as Google AdSense to fund platform hosting costs.

Ad networks may use web cookies, web beacons, or tracking technologies to serve relevant ads based on prior website visits. Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or by adjusting your web browser cookie preferences.

4. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Compliance Rights

We respect international data privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

The Right to Access & Erasure: Because we do not harvest or store personal identity databases or user file payloads, we do not hold personal profile records to disclose or delete.

Do Not Sell My Personal Information: Browser Tools Hub does not sell, rent, or trade user data or usage records to third-party data brokers.

5. External Web Links

Our website may contain hyperlinked references to external sites. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party domains. We advise users to review the privacy documentation of any external platform they visit.

6. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page alongside a revised "Last Updated" timestamp. Continual use of our services after policy updates indicates acceptance of revised terms.
